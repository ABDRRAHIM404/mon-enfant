# Phase 5 appointments and continuity contract

Implements PROJECT_PLAN.md and USER_STORIES.md D09/D10/D11 using fictional records. Clinical/source/hosting/privacy sign-off remains external; no catch-up protocol is defined here.

- Appointment identity fixes child, responsible doctor, declared facility, purpose and optional exact vaccine-item reference. Appointment management requires its own current `appointment` capability, child grant, approved doctor and live session. It confers no clinical-record capability. Only the responsible doctor may change the appointment; other authorized treating doctors may read child appointment history.
- Each creation, reschedule, cancellation, completed visit or missed disposition appends an immutable, expected-revision event with actor/platform ID, timestamp, reason and old/new times. Dates are explicit instants rendered in Africa/Casablanca. Reject nonexistent local times and require a disambiguating offset for repeated local times. No silent timezone or catch-up-date guessing.
- A scheduled slot whose end has passed is a missed appointment until a disposition is recorded. Reading may derive that flag. Before changing a past scheduled slot, persist its missed snapshot, then append the clinician's chosen disposition, retaining the original slot and observer/author. A reschedule never inserts an administration or fulfills an intended dose.
- Vaccination appointments require an active, reviewed child-specific intended item. One open appointment per responsible doctor/item prevents duplicate reminders. Another authorized doctor's validated administration resolves the vaccine follow-up projection from the committed clinical ledger. This does not assert attendance at the original appointment or cancel an unrelated consultation. Show the qualifying professional/date/reference and retain all raw appointment events.
- A corrected/withdrawn administration immediately changes the projection; the original outstanding follow-up can reopen. Cancelled appointments remain cancelled. A withdrawn intended reference requires review, not a claim that the dose was administered. Manual visit completion is a disposition for non-vaccination appointments; vaccine completion requires the actual validated administration.
- Routine reference dates/windows retain the version, exact-birth/eligibility gates and month-end/leap-year rules. Potentially overdue/unconfirmed reference, unknown history and a confirmed missed appointment are distinct. Only actual appointments produce a missed-appointment state. No unassigned dose or missing historical record is invented as a missed vaccination.
- Calendar and queues use current child grants, explicitly paged results/totals and minimal authorized details. Shared child history is audited. Derive follow-up flags on each fresh read to avoid durable stale reminder rows; visible calendar/worklist refreshes must be explicit and bounded. JSON/print exports include all appointment identities/events within explicit interactive limits.

Acceptance requires both the A/125–B/78 cross-doctor scenario (independent consultation retained) and missed vaccination → manual reschedule → pending dose scenario, including retries, concurrent/stale decisions, correction/withdrawal reopening, timezone/month boundaries and access/capability/session revocation. Automated and hosted software evidence does not replace qualified clinical review.
